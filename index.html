About Me

I'm Alex, a systems developer who cares about software that does its job reliably, every time.

I like the parts of a system most people never see: parsing and validating data, keeping records consistent, getting the calculations right, and storing it all safely. I work across COBOL, Java, C and C++, use LLMs in my day-to-day development workflow, and am drawn to the business-critical systems that quietly keep things running.

Before software I worked in audio engineering and welding/fabrication, trades where precision and process matter and mistakes are expensive. I bring the same habits to code: measure twice, test thoroughly, and leave it clear enough for the next person to pick up.
